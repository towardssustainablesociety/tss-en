## Why was TSS created?

I have worked in IT since 1969 and long believed that automation would reduce the need for work. It did not turn out that way. We created new jobs instead, and economic growth became a goal in itself. In the 1980s or 1990s my son, then a teenager, said that work was overrated. The thought stayed with me.

More questions grew out of it. Why does the economy always have to grow? What does that do to nature? And who is responsible for the whole?

## Where do the ideas come from?

The frame for the whole comes from the UN's sustainability areas (SDGs) and from Doughnut Economics. They showed that there were similar ideas internationally, and they gave my own vision a firmer foundation. A book that takes a broad view is *Earth for All* by Johan Rockström and others.

## How did TSS come about?

After retiring in 2014, I began writing about these questions on social media and to the media and opinion-formers. Around 2018 I was a member of four political parties for a year, to understand politics from the inside. There was no response.

In 2024 I began asking ChatGPT questions, and in 2025 this became the report series *Mot ett hållbart samhälle* (Towards a Sustainable Society, in Swedish). It laid the foundation for TSS. The texts here have been written afresh with the support of ChatGPT and Claude, in Swedish first and then in English. I have reviewed and approved every page, and factual claims that can be measured have a source.

## Is there an agenda?

Only the vision: **a good life for all, within planetary boundaries**. TSS is not an organisation and not a campaign. The site has no funding and no advertising, and does not take sides with any party. It describes how society works and which paths there are, not which path is the right one.

## Have I got it right?

Not necessarily. I am not a researcher, and someone trying to see the whole may miss something in the details. If you see something that is wrong, or something that is missing, I would very much like to know. Get in touch via [Contact](<../Contact/Contact.md>).
