**Do get in touch!**

Have you found something that is wrong? Is there a question missing, or do you know of a source that ought to be included? Or do you just want to say what you think? Write to <towards.sustainable.life@gmail.com>.

If it is about a mistake, it is easier to correct if you say which page it concerns and, if you have one, a source.

TSS is made by one person in their spare time, so it may take a while before you get a reply. But everything that comes in is read. You are welcome to write in English or Swedish.
