## Welcome!

Would you like to live in a sustainable and secure society?

Most people would probably say yes to that question. A society where everyone can live a good life, within planetary boundaries, is something that people with very different views can get behind.

Creating such a society takes time. It is work that stretches over several decades and requires efforts from many people and organisations. It becomes easier if we have a shared picture of where we are heading. A [vision of a sustainable society](<../Our Society/The Vision/The Vision.md>) can give that direction to our decisions and our work for change.

**Towards Sustainable Society – TSS** is a knowledge site that helps you understand how our society works, how its different parts affect each other and how we can together contribute to a more sustainable society. TSS does not take sides with any party. It describes how society works and which paths there are, not which path is the right one.

The picture at the top is [the Society Map](<../Our Society/The Society Map/The Society Map.md>). It shows the four societal systems with people at the centre, all within planetary boundaries. Every part of the map is a link.

If you want to understand the basics, you can start with [Our Society](<../Our Society/Our Society.md>). There the picture of society is built up step by step.

If you would rather go straight to a question that interests you, you can start in [the Society Explorer](<../Society Explorer/Society Explorer.md>).

If you want to know who is behind TSS and why the site exists, you can read [About TSS](<../About TSS/About TSS.md>).
