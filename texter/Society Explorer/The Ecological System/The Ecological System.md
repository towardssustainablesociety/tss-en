The ecological system is about nature and the processes that make life possible: ecosystems, species, water, soil, air and climate. People are part of the system and at the same time entirely dependent on it working.

Nature gives us food, water, materials and energy, and it takes care of much of what we leave behind. At the same time, society changes nature through how we produce, consume and use land and resources. The ecological system therefore sets the limits for what is possible in every other part of society.

But when you try to understand society's relationship with nature, questions arise:

- Are we really that dependent on nature?

- How bad is the state of the planet, really?

- Are we running out of resources?

- Can't technology solve the problems?

- And what does it have to do with me?

This is where TSS starts. Each group gathers questions that belong together and tries to give answers that make sense. The answers describe how nature works, how society affects it and what possibilities for change there are.

Choose a question that interests you from the list at the end of the page, and start there.
