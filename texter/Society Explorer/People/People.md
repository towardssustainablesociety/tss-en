## Why are People at the centre of the Society Map?

Because society exists for people, and it is people who shape it. A sustainable society is fundamentally about everyone being able to live a good life, within planetary boundaries.

People are not a fifth societal system. They are present in all four. We are part of nature and live from it. We work, buy and own in the economy. We have relationships, norms and culture in the social system. And we choose, influence and decide together in the democratic system.

That is why society cannot be understood without understanding people, nor people without society.

## How does society shape our lives?

More than we usually think. The societal systems decide what **living conditions** we have and how much **room for action** we have, meaning what we can actually choose between.

Nature gives us food, water and a climate to live in. The economy decides which jobs exist and what things cost. Norms and culture affect what we see as a successful life. Laws and political decisions determine what rights we have and who gets to take part in deciding.

Two people with the same abilities can therefore have very different lives depending on where and when they are born. That is described in more detail in [Living Conditions and Chances](<../The Social and Cultural System/Living Conditions and Chances/Living Conditions and Chances.md>) in the Social and Cultural System.

## How do we shape society?

Through the roles we have. The same person is a citizen and voter, a consumer, an employee or business owner, a parent, a neighbour and perhaps a member of an association. In each role we make decisions that, together with other people's decisions, shape society.

Taken one by one, the decisions look small. But that is how norms change, markets shift and political majorities are formed. Change often looks impossible for a long time and then happens fast once enough people have begun. That is described in more detail in [Norms, Values and Ideals](<../The Social and Cultural System/Norms, Values and Ideals/Norms, Values and Ideals.md>) in the Social and Cultural System.

## Isn't it enough for each of us to do the right thing?

No, not on its own. It is a common and reasonable thought: if everyone just takes responsibility for their own life, the rest will sort itself out. And it is true that individual choices matter, both for the footprint and for showing that something else is possible.

But our choices are made within frameworks that others have set. Someone who wants to travel sustainably, eat sustainably or work with something sustainable depends on it existing, being affordable and being easy to choose. When the sustainable choice is more expensive or more complicated, it becomes the exception, however much people want it.

That is why a great deal of responsibility lies with society. Nobody should have to choose between making a living and contributing to a sustainable society. The individual's most important contribution is perhaps not what they buy, but taking part in changing the frameworks, as a voter, at work and in public debate.

## Does it matter where you act?

Yes, but the same questions apply everywhere. The Society Map applies at every **level of society**, from the household to the world.

A household thinking about a car, a home or a holiday touches all four systems, just like a municipality planning a new housing area or a country reforming its tax system. The difference is how much room for action there is and how far the consequences reach.

What the levels are, and what can be influenced at each of them, is described in [Levels of Society](<Levels of Society/Levels of Society.md>).

## How can I use the Society Map myself?

As a checklist when a question feels big or complicated. Ask yourself which of the four systems are affected, what dependencies there are between them and what consequences a decision can have in the other parts of society. Also ask yourself whether the proposal stays within planetary boundaries and whether it gives everyone a place above the social foundation. A worked example, the journey to work, can be found in [Levels of Society](<Levels of Society/Levels of Society.md>).

Questions about society almost always touch several systems at once. It is easy to miss something when you only see your own part. AI can be a support for exploring connections, actors and possible consequences from several perspectives, and the Society Map can then work as a structure for the question so that no important parts are forgotten.

## So what is the answer, really?

People are shaped by society and shape it at the same time. The societal systems set the frameworks for our lives, at every level from the household to the world, but it is people who built the frameworks and people who can change them.

Individual choices matter, but they are not enough. What decides the matter is whether we together build a society where the sustainable choice is the easy choice.

**The question is not whether it is people or the system that must change, but how we as people change the systems together.**
