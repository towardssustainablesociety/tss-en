The Society Explorer is the way in for those who want to go deep into a particular question. Here society is divided into the same parts as in the Society Map: four societal systems, People, who are present in all the systems, and the Framework, which describes what a sustainable society requires.

Each societal system is described with questions and answers. The questions are ones many people ask themselves, and the answers try to be direct: how things are, why they are that way, whether they have to be that way and what could be different. The most common objection often gets a question of its own.

If you want to see the whole first, you can start with [Our Society](<../Our Society/Our Society.md>). There the picture of society is built up step by step.

## The parts of society

- [The Democratic System](<The Democratic System/The Democratic System.md>) – how we decide together.

- [The Ecological System](<The Ecological System/The Ecological System.md>) – the nature that everything else rests on.

- [The Economic System](<The Economic System/The Economic System.md>) – how we organise work, production and distribution.

- [The Social and Cultural System](<The Social and Cultural System/The Social and Cultural System.md>) – relationships, norms, culture and trust.

- [People](<People/People.md>) – we are shaped by society and shape it at the same time.

- [Framework](<Framework/Framework.md>) – the UN's sustainability areas (SDGs), Doughnut Economics and the planetary boundaries.
