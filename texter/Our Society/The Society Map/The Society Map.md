## What does the map show?

The Society Map is a simplified picture of society. It shows the four societal systems, how they fit together and which limits they must stay within. Every part of the map is a link to the page that explains it. The underlined names are links, just as in the text. The colours distinguish the systems, but it is the underlining that shows where you can click.

## Why are people at the centre?

Because society exists for people, and it is people who shape it. People are not a fifth system. They are present in all four. There is more about this on the page [People](<../../Society Explorer/People/People.md>).

## What does the ring around people mean?

The ring comes from Doughnut Economics. The lower half is the **social foundation**: what every person needs to live with dignity. The upper half is the **ecological ceiling**: the limits that humanity needs to stay within so as not to damage the Earth's life-support systems. A good life for all, within planetary boundaries, fits between the foundation and the ceiling.

## What are the four boxes?

They are the four societal systems. The ecological system provides the conditions for life. The economic system stands for livelihoods and the flows of resources. The democratic system stands for governance, power and legitimacy. The social and cultural system holds people's needs, values and relationships.

The arrows show that the systems affect each other. A change in one system almost always has consequences in the others. How that happens is shown with examples on the page [Our Society](<../Our Society.md>).

## What is the outer ring?

It is the planetary boundaries: the nine processes in the Earth system that together keep the Earth stable and that are affected by human activity. Everything in society happens inside them. How they stand today is described in [State of the Planet – The Ecological System](<../../Society Explorer/The Ecological System/State of the Planet/State of the Planet.md>).

## Where does the map come from?

The map builds on the knowledge in the Framework, at the bottom of the picture: the UN's sustainability areas (SDGs), Doughnut Economics and the planetary boundaries. The Framework describes what sustainability requires. The map shows where in society it has to be achieved.

## Does the map only apply to a whole country?

No. The same four systems exist at every level where people live and decide together, from the household and the association to the municipality, the state, the EU and the world. That is described on the page [Levels of Society – People](<../../Society Explorer/People/Levels of Society/Levels of Society.md>).

**The map does not answer what should be done, but it shows where to look.**
